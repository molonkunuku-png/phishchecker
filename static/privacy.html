Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-18

PhishChecker is a privacy-first scanning tool. We do not store any URLs you scan, nor do we collect personal information beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

Data handling

  • Scans are processed transiently and are not retained after analysis.
  • No account creation or personal identifiers are required.
  • Telemetry is limited to aggregate operational metrics and does not include scan contents.
